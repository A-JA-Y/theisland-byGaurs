  Gaurs Group has opened select bookings for The Islands by Gaurs, a 506-unit condominium development set inside the 452-acre Jaypee Greens township at Pari Chowk, Greater Noida. The project spans 9 towers rising up to 37 storeys, with only two residences per floor — each opening onto the 18-hole Greg Norman-designed golf course on one side and the Pari Chowk skyline on the other.

  ## What's on offer
  <a id="offer"></a>

  The Islands by Gaurs is being launched across 4, 5 and 6 BHK configurations. Saleable areas span approximately 3,595 sq.ft. in the boutique Tower E layout to 5,962 sq.ft. in the flagship Tower A Premium residences, alongside a 5 BHK configuration in Blocks H-01/I-01 and a curated 6 BHK / duplex / penthouse tier available by invitation.

  ## Amenities across three zones
  <a id="amenities"></a>

  The project's amenities are organised across The Ivory Coast, an open-air ground-level activity zone; The Gold Coast, a 30,000 sq.ft. clubhouse with an indoor heated pool, spa and dining options; and The Orbit, a skywalk 90 metres above the ground connecting 8 of the 9 towers with a paddle court, jogging track and observatory deck.

  ## Location inside an established township
  <a id="location"></a>

  Unlike greenfield launches, The Islands by Gaurs sits inside a township that is already operating — Jaypee Greens' golf course, schools, hospitals and retail are functioning today. The address is close to the Pari Chowk and Alpha 1 metro stations, the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, and is roughly 30 minutes from the upcoming Noida International Airport at Jewar via the Yamuna Expressway.
