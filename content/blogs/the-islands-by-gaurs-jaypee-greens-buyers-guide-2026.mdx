## About The Islands by Gaurs
<a id="about"></a>

The Islands by Gaurs is a curated enclave of 506 ultra-luxury condominiums inside Jaypee Greens, arranged across 9 towers in a single sweeping boulevard at Pari Chowk, Greater Noida. Every residence opens to the 18-hole Greg Norman-designed golf course on one side and the Pari Chowk skyline on the other — and with only two condominiums per floor, there is no compromise on privacy.
