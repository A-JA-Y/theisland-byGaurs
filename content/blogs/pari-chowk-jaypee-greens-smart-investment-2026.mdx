## An Established Township, Not a Greenfield Bet
<a id="established"></a>

A large share of investment risk in Indian real estate comes from buying into a location before its infrastructure exists — roads, schools, hospitals and retail that are all "coming soon." Jaypee Greens removes much of that uncertainty. The 452-acre township, its 18-hole Greg Norman-designed golf course, schools, hospitals and the Jaypee Greens Town Centre are already operating. The Islands by Gaurs is being built inside that established ecosystem, not ahead of it.

<Callout type="info">
  Buying inside a delivered township changes the nature of the decision — from "will the infrastructure arrive" to "how do I want to live inside infrastructure that already exists."
</Callout>

## The Connectivity Triangle
<a id="connectivity"></a>

Pari Chowk sits at the intersection of three of the NCR's most consequential growth corridors: the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, the Yamuna Expressway, and — a short drive further — the upcoming Noida International Airport at Jewar.

An upcoming international airport of Jewar's scale is a rare, once-in-a-generation infrastructure event for any region. It doesn't guarantee outcomes for any single project, but it is a genuine structural tailwind for the wider Pari Chowk–Yamuna Expressway corridor that buyers should weigh into their own research.
